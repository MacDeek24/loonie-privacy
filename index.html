🛡️ Our Privacy Guarantee in Brief

Family Ledger is built offline-first. We operate no central servers, no cloud tracking, and no behavioral analytics. Your financial transactions, member names, budgets, and scanned receipts remain strictly on your device. When you opt to backup your data, it connects directly from your phone to your personal Google Drive account. We never sell, rent, or see your data.

01 Information Collection & Storage Architecture

Unlike traditional financial apps, Family Ledger does not require you to create an account with us, nor does it upload your records to any developer-managed server.

📱 Local SQLite/Room Database

All transactions, custom categories, salary division rules, budgets, and member profiles are encrypted and stored in your device's private sandbox storage.

🚫 Zero Server Footprint

We do not own, rent, or operate backend application servers that receive, harvest, or aggregate your financial transactions.

🔐 Hardware-Backed PIN

App lock PINs are salted with cryptographically secure random bytes and hashed using Android Keystore HMAC-SHA256, resistant to offline brute-force attacks.

02 Device Permissions & How They Are Used

Family Ledger requests only the minimum operating system permissions strictly necessary to deliver its core features:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA):
    Used exclusively for our Smart Bill & Receipt Scanner. The camera stream is analyzed on-device using Google ML Kit Text Recognition. Captured images are processed entirely on your phone’s local processor, are never transmitted over the internet, and are automatically deleted immediately after text extraction (or purged within 1 hour).
  • Biometric Sensor (android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC):
    Allows you to unlock Family Ledger using your fingerprint or device facial recognition. Your biometric identifiers are handled entirely by Android’s secure hardware (TEE/StrongBox); Family Ledger never accesses or stores your raw biometric data.
  • Notifications (android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS):
    Used solely to deliver your opted-in daily check-in reminders (e.g., daily allowance status). Notifications are marked private (VISIBILITY_PRIVATE) to ensure sensitive dollar amounts are never visible on your lock screen.

03 Google Drive Cloud Backup (Optional)

You may choose to connect your Google account to back up and restore your ledger data across devices.

  • Restricted Scope (drive.appdata):
    Family Ledger uses the official Google Drive AppData folder scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata). This scope strictly isolates the app's backup files within a private, hidden folder in your Google Drive. Family Ledger cannot read, view, or modify any other files in your personal Google Drive.
  • Peer-to-Google Architecture:
    Backup files are transferred directly between your Android phone and Google's servers over secure TLS/HTTPS. The developer has zero intermediary servers, zero access keys, and zero ability to inspect your backup contents.
  • Complete Deletion Rights:
    You can delete all cloud backups at any time directly within the app using the "Delete all cloud backups" button in Backup Settings, or by disconnecting the app in your Google Account security dashboard.

04 Third-Party Tracking, Analytics & Advertising

We respect your privacy by omitting the tracking infrastructure found in common mobile applications:

  • No Third-Party Advertising: Family Ledger contains zero advertising SDKs. We do not monetize your data or display ads.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not embed analytics trackers (such as Facebook Pixel, Adjust, or AppsFlyer) to monitor your browsing or spending habits.
  • No Data Brokering: We never sell, lease, exchange, or share your personal, financial, or behavioral information with data brokers or marketing firms.

05 Data Retention, Export & Deletion Rights

You maintain 100% sovereign ownership and control over your financial records at all times:

  • Exporting Data: You can export your complete ledger records into standard, unencrypted CSV files at any time via the Settings screen.
  • Local Reset: You can erase all local databases, categories, transactions, and settings by clicking "Reset All Data" in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App: Because data is stored in the application's private sandbox, uninstalling Family Ledger from your Android device immediately and permanently deletes all local records.

06 Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Family Ledger is designed for adults and families to manage household expenses and budgets. The application is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).

07 Security Safeguards

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your financial data on-device:

  • Recents Screen Protection: Window security flags (FLAG_SECURE) and Android 13+ recents snapshot prevention are automatically engaged when app PIN lock is active.
  • Non-Destructive Recovery: Database schema downgrades and recovery operations are protected by durable transactional state machines to ensure power loss never corrupts your financial history.
  • Encrypted Transport: All cloud communications with Google Drive API utilize modern TLS 1.3 encryption.
